Appointments and hosted research

I am looking for the institutional home for an independent research programme on information sufficiency for structural decisions: a professorship in structural mechanics, numerical methods or structural reliability; a hosted research project or a visiting appointment; or a research-lead role in industry.

Collaboration

Joint research on inverse problems, reliability and data-informed assessment — including joint proposals, co-supervision of doctoral work, and lectures. If you are working on identifiability, monitoring-based assessment or nonlinear reliability, I would like to hear from you.
